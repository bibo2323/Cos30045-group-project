Data preparation & all sources

BITRE hospitalised injury data, September 2023; underlying AIHW National Hospital Morbidity Database.

National and state traffic hospitalisations are totalled and checked against published tables. First Nations figures use published annual totals because some raw age cells are suppressed.

Counts are not population-adjusted risk. Unpublished values are missing, never zero.
